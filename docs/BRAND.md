# Brand

1. **Código Local**: local, close to the neighborhood business, technical but friendly.
2. **Nexo Web Studio**: connecting businesses with their customers online.
3. **Taller Digital**: a workshop that builds digital tools; fits well with talleres and constructoras.

Decision pending. All copy below uses `[Nombre]` as a placeholder.

## Tone of voice
- Clear and direct. No jargon (avoid words like "stack", "framework", "SEO técnico" in client copy).
- Close and respectful: use "usted" by default, which is standard for business in Colombia.
- Results-focused: talk about more customers, more calls, more WhatsApp messages, not about technology.
- Honest: no invented numbers, clients, or prices.
