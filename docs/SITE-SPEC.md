# Site Spec: Agency Website

## Base
The agency site is based on Daniel's web developer portfolio:
- Live: https://portfoliodanielsantoyo.netlify.app/
- Repo: https://github.com/daftboston/NewPortfolio

It will be **rebranded under the company name** (see BRAND.md): the personal portfolio becomes the studio's site, with copy aimed at business owners instead of recruiters.

This document covers pages and content only. Technical decisions are out of scope here.

## Language
Spanish (Colombia).

## Pages

### 1. Inicio
- Hero with main message and WhatsApp button.
- Short list of who we help: constructoras, talleres, ópticas.
- Preview of 2–3 portfolio projects.
- Final call to action.

### 2. Servicios
- Página web para su negocio (diseño y publicación).
- Catálogo en línea (productos o servicios, con contacto por WhatsApp).
- Mantenimiento y actualizaciones.
- No prices on the site for now (pricing and offer are defined by Mr market).

### 3. Portafolio
- Cards for each project in PORTFOLIO.md: image, short description, link.
- Demos labeled clearly as "Demo".

### 4. Proceso
Simple steps, for example:
1. Conversamos sobre su negocio.
2. Le mostramos una propuesta o demo.
3. Construimos su página.
4. Publicamos y le damos soporte.

### 5. Nosotros
- Who we are, based on Daniel's portfolio bio, written in the studio's voice.
- Focus on Colombian local businesses.

### 6. Contacto / WhatsApp
- WhatsApp button with prefilled message, e.g.:
  > Hola, vi su página y quiero información sobre una página web para mi negocio.
- Simple contact form (name, business, phone, message).
- Floating WhatsApp button on all pages.
